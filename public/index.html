NEON DRIFT
A rogue signal has flooded the circuit-city with corrupted riders. You are the last sentinel cycle tasked with restoring the grid—outmaneuver the hunters, reclaim energy nodes, and crash the overlord before the neon horizon collapses.
Rules
  • Survive all 10 rounds inside the arena.
  • Crash enemy bikes while dodging walls and your own trail.
  • Collect power cores to charge weapons and earn bonuses.
Gridcraft Tips
  • Feint near barriers, then snap across to trap pursuers.
  • Chain power-ups before engaging corrupted riders.
  • Keep sight-lines open when the arena pulses and shrinks.
Controls WASD / Arrow Keys steer · Space pauses · Click “Mute” to silence the synths
